Thief RT -- credits and licences
================================

What this disk image contains, who made it, and under what terms. The licences
themselves are in the Licences folder.

The Dark Engine, restored (the game engine)
  Its contributors. MIT-style licence (Licences/Dark Engine Restored (MIT).txt).
  Thief RT is that engine built for Apple silicon Macs with a path-traced renderer and
  an OpenGL renderer; its source as built is Source/thief-rt-engine-src.tar.gz, and
  Source/setup.sh builds it (with the libraries below).

RTGL1 (the path tracer)
  Sultim Tsyrendashiev and contributors. MIT License.
  https://github.com/sultim-t/RayTracedGL1
  Thief RT's version is RTGL1's Metal port with the changes in Source/patches/rtgl1-lib.

Steam Audio (traced sound: reverb and occlusion from the level's walls)
  Valve Corporation. Apache License 2.0. https://github.com/ValveSoftware/steam-audio

Libraries inside the engine
  SDL 2 (zlib licence), OpenAL Soft (GNU LGPL 2; a separate library, in
  Engine/Thief RT.app/Contents/Frameworks), FFmpeg (GNU LGPL 2.1; the cutscenes),
  Ogg and Vorbis (BSD licences), libpng (libpng licence), Squirrel (MIT).
  FFmpeg is linked into the engine: Source/setup.sh builds it from its own source and
  links the engine with it again, so a changed FFmpeg can be used.

Not included (the installer finds the player's own copies)
  Thief: The Dark Project, Thief Gold and Thief II: The Metal Age (Looking Glass
  Studios; Eidos, now Square Enix): the games' data.
  The Black Parade and other fan missions (their authors), the Thief Gold and Thief II
  HD Mods (Bentraxx) and other mods (their authors).
